Email Troubleshooting Guide

Issue:
Users cannot access email, receive authentication prompts, or cannot send and receive messages.

Troubleshooting Procedure:

1. Confirm that the device has an active network connection.
2. Check that the mail service is reachable.
3. Confirm that the mailbox is within its storage limit.
4. Verify account authentication and any required multi-factor authentication.
5. Restart the mail client and attempt to reconnect.
6. Check whether the issue affects one user or multiple users.

Escalation Criteria:

Escalate to the Messaging Team when authentication is valid but the mailbox or
mail service remains inaccessible, or when multiple users are affected.

Recommended Escalation Team:
Messaging Team.
