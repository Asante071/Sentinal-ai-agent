Printer Troubleshooting Guide

Issue:
Users cannot print, the printer is offline, or documents remain in the print queue.

Troubleshooting Procedure:

1. Confirm that the printer is powered on and has no hardware error indicator.
2. Confirm the printer is online and reachable on the network.
3. Check the print queue for stalled or failed jobs.
4. Clear only the affected stalled jobs and retry a test page.
5. Confirm the print spooler service is running; restart it if policy allows.
6. Reconnect the approved printer queue if the issue continues.

Escalation Criteria:

Escalate to the Print Services Team when a printer remains unreachable, repeated
queue failures affect multiple users, or a hardware fault is indicated.

Recommended Escalation Team:
Print Services Team.
