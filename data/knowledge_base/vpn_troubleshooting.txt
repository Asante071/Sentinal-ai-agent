VPN Troubleshooting Guide

Issue:
Users are unable to connect to the corporate VPN and receive authentication errors.

Troubleshooting Procedure:

1. Confirm that the user has an active internet connection.

2. Verify that the VPN client is installed and running.

3. Confirm that the user's corporate credentials are valid.

4. Check whether multi-factor authentication (MFA) is functioning correctly.

5. Restart the VPN client and attempt authentication again.

6. If authentication continues to fail while the VPN gateway, authentication service, and MFA service are reachable, the incident should be escalated to the Identity and Access Team.

Escalation Criteria:

Escalate the incident when:
- User authentication continues to fail.
- The VPN gateway is reachable.
- The authentication service is reachable.
- The MFA service is reachable.

Recommended Escalation Team:
Identity and Access Team.
