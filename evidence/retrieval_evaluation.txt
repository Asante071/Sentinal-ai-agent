SentinelAI - knowledge-base retrieval evaluation (evidence for report, Parts D and E)
Date:    2026-10-08
Tool:    eval_retrieval.py (real embedding model, no Gemini involved)
Model:   all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (sentence-transformers), vectors stored in ChromaDB
Corpus:  12 knowledge-base articles (Mmabotse): 3 per category (VPN, Account, Email, Printer)
Chunking: one chunk per article (articles are about 100 words); Article ID, category, title and file kept as metadata
Search:  top 3 articles returned, closest first (distance = squared Euclidean, lower is closer)

QUESTION
Does the search find the right article for a plain-language problem description?
Two ways of indexing an article were compared:
  full     the whole article text is embedded (current default)
  summary  only title + keywords + symptoms are embedded (the full article is still what is returned)

RESULT (15 hand-written questions, one expected article each)
  mode      right article first    right article in top 3
  full      14 / 15                15 / 15
  summary   14 / 15                15 / 15

  Only miss, in both modes:
  "VPN restart failed and the gateway did not accept the session"  expected KB-VPN-001
     returned order: KB-VPN-003, KB-VPN-001, KB-VPN-002
     (the wording "did not accept the session" is close in meaning to "VPN rejects username or password")

DECISION
  The summary mode gave no improvement, so the simpler default (full article) is kept.
  Returning 3 results, not 1, matters: the right article was always among them, including for the one miss.

LIMITATIONS
  - Small test set (15 questions) written by one author after reading the articles, so the score is probably
    optimistic. It is an indicative check, not a benchmark. Questions written by someone else, ideally taken from
    real tickets, would be a better test.
  - It measures retrieval only. It does not measure whether the agent then follows the retrieved article.
  - A question outside the knowledge base (a request for a new laptop) was closest to its best article at distance
    1.84, while in-scope questions were between 0.38 and 0.89 in the first check. This suggests a distance cut-off
    could work (setting KB_MAX_DISTANCE). It was chosen from only 8 questions. It is off by default in the code, but a value of 1.5
    is set in the .env on the author's machine (the date it was added is not recorded). The 15-question test above does not use
    the cut-off, so its numbers are not affected; the agent's own searches may be.
