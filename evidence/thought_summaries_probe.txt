SentinelAI - measuring when Gemini returns its reasoning (evidence for report, Part D design decision)
Date:    2026-10-09
Tools:   probe_thoughts.py (structure of Gemini's replies) and probe_thoughts2.py (frequency by thinking level)
Model:   gemini-3.5-flash-lite
Purpose: the brief names the thought-action-observation cycle. The agent already showed actions and observations;
         this checks how to show the "thought" from Gemini's own reasoning, not from invented text.

FINDING 1 (probe_thoughts.py): what Gemini returns by default
  Each reply contains a step of type "thought" followed by one or more "function_call" steps. By default the thought
  step carries only an encrypted signature and no readable text. Asking only for summaries
  (thinking_summaries = "auto") also returned no text in the sample taken. With a thinking level set as well
  (thinking_level = "low", thinking_summaries = "auto") a readable summary came back in that sample, and Gemini
  asked for two tools at once (parallel calls), which the agent's loop already handles.

FINDING 2 (probe_thoughts2.py): how often a readable summary comes back
  Three typical messages (a VPN fault, a VPN restart that fails, a printer hardware fault) at three thinking levels.
  level    message   seconds   summary characters
  low      1          10.8       0       (no summary)
  low      2           8.3     520
  low      3           6.9       0       (no summary)
  medium   1          12.7     598
  medium   2          11.8     717
  medium   3           7.0     799
  high     1           5.5    1092
  high     2          12.0     749
  high     3           8.9     753
  Summaries returned:  low 1 of 3,   medium 3 of 3,   high 3 of 3.
  The agent's own reader extracted the text correctly from the real replies.

DECISION
  Thought summaries are requested with thinking_level = "medium" (reliable in the sample, and not the most expensive
  level). They are shown as [THOUGHT] lines before each action, and recorded in the run's event log.
  If Gemini returns no summary for a step, none is shown; no text is invented.

EFFECT ON THE MEASURED RESULTS
  The three final evaluation runs were made with thoughts OFF (default for the evaluation).
  One extra run with thoughts ON at thinking_level = "low" (lite_thoughts_run1) scored 14/15, 0 false escalations,
  S08 failing as before, 16.5 s per scenario: no regression. A run at the final setting ("medium") is still to be made.

LIMITATIONS
  - Three calls per level on one model on one day: it shows a pattern, not a guarantee.
  - Time per call varied from 5.5 s to 12.7 s with no clear link to the thinking level; with this few calls, the
    differences are within normal variation.
  - The summaries are written by the model after thinking. They describe its reasoning but are not a literal transcript.
  - Summaries are long (about 500 to 1100 characters) and informal ("Okay, so..."); the console shows the first 500.
