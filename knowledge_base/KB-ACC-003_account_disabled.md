# Account disabled

**Article ID:** KB-ACC-003
**Category:** Account
**Keywords:** account disabled, deactivated, access removed

## Symptoms
The user reports being unable to sign in and the account status is DISABLED.

## Resolution steps
1. Check the account status (tool: check_account_status).
2. Explain that disabled accounts cannot be re-enabled through self-service.
3. Create a ticket with the reason the user gives (tool: create_support_ticket) and escalate it (tool: escalate_incident).

## When to escalate
Always escalate. Never attempt reset_password on a disabled account.
