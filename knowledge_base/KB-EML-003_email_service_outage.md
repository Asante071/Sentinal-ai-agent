# Email service outage

**Article ID:** KB-EML-003
**Category:** Email
**Keywords:** email down, exchange, service unavailable, all users

## Symptoms
Several users report they cannot use email at the same time.

## Resolution steps
1. Check email service health for the affected user (tool: check_email_service).
2. If the service is not up, do not try local fixes.
3. Create a high-priority ticket and escalate immediately.

## When to escalate
Always escalate service-wide outages.
